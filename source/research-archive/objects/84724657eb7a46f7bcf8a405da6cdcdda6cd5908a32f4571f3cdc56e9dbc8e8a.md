# Scope and hypotheses before timing

Baseline: c32-owner-layout-20261025/plan.txt (C32 natural FFN plus preserved F20-linear). This baseline is immutable. Candidate only redirects the seven C32 SPV paths; weights, F20, grids, PUSH, buffers and output ABI remain unchanged.

Naive whole-QKV reorientation is not a free removal. Keeping the existing norm tree requires two16x16 transpose networks for Q and two for K:40 packed shuffles. The original Y layout remains needed by the final projection residual seed, another20. Even allowing V direct publication to its shared layout, this concrete variant costs60 instead of current28 at these boundaries. This rejects that particular implementation, not all possible QKV/owner redesigns. Rewriting norm ownership may change this accounting but needs an independently demonstrated ordered reduction and full lifetime accounting.

Implemented narrower hypothesis: current network computes butterfly intermediates -> Y owner permutation -> B owner permutation. The new network fans out both Y and B directly from the butterfly intermediates. The QKV B input no longer waits on the last Y permutation. The residual still receives exactly the same Y coordinates; no cost is moved into norm, attention, residual or SLM. The source shares one packed uint for both consumers, removing eight source repacks per subgroup. Whether the compiler already removes them, and whether parallel fanout reduces native work or merely changes allocation, must be measured.

Proof: after three butterfly stages, v[l][d] contains (token=(l&14)+(l>>4), feature=(l&1)+2*d). Y source lane sy=(l&14)|((l&1)<<4)|(l>>4). B source lane sb=(l&1)|((l&28)>>1)|((l&2)<<3). For every lane0..31 and component0..7, sy yields the original CH transpose coordinate and sb yields (token=l>>1, feature=2*d+(l&1)), the measured B mapping. All512 coordinates asserted in build.py. No floating arithmetic is inserted into the transport.

Accounting before native:28 packed shuffles remains28,72 half selects remains72. No DPAS, rounding, reduction order, shared byte or barrier changes. Four residual CH matrices remain alive as before. A common packed source may be alive until both consumers complete; do not assume lower physical register peak. Native scratch sends must remain zero before ordinary tests. Native max-r is not GRF allocation. Ordinary tests compare this candidate against the current combined baseline, never against older COMBO-1.
