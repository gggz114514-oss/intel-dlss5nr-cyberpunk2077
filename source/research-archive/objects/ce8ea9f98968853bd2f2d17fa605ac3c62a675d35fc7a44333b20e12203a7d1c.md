Delete6144half=12288B shared read requests/WG (201437184B/frame at169×97), with no new/repeated QKV MMA or logical weight loads. Retain16B/lane input halves until final QKV region. ## Whole-family long-lived values and limits

F01: y00/y01/y10 register residuals cross attention; y11 explicitly lives in1KB SLM until output seed after the verified scratch repair. Q/K/V/pool choices share this entry's much larger feature code, so seven-body allocation cannot be assumed.

Seven body: perSG16query×32features, Y residual crosses attention; Q retained locally, K/V published acrossSG; P survives its den tree before PV. Shared K tile consumed by both query8 fragments is useful reuse. Splitting those owners may double K traffic; no such change is justified without accounting.

F2: scalar blend owner now feeds natural matrix fragments through16 packed shuffles/SG. Projection shK still needs publication and reader retirement before K reuse. Its shorter local input lifetime does not imply the F01 feature producer can be copied unchanged.

F20: shY remains4KB from contract through output, intentionally avoids a long register residual; shArena expansion/QKV share storage and therefore enforce true anti-dependencies. PV->output already uses sameowner register fragments, making its old normal barrier removable. The new contract->QKV mapping only retains16B/lane for that nearby consumer; it does not retain all Y across attention. Across-network rule: choose layout and owner per edge, then check allocation reuse dependencies separately from value dependencies.
