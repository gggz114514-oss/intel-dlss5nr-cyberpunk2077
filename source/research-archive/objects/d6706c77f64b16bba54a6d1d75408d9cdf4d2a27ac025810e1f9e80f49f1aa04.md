## 1. What has to be measured, and what the harness can resolve

The decision the probe has to support (card, verbatim): *is one fragment transport worth
≈0.005 ms (then the remaining 48 cross-SG transports, 384 messages, are worth ≈0.24 ms and a
layout redesign deserves F20's share) or ≈0.001 ms (then the whole direction is worth
≈0.05 ms and should be closed)?*

The available measurement instruments and their resolution:

| instrument | design | resolution actually available | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gates` fixed / fb2 | 1–2 whole frames | frame gold required; whole-frame only | `COMMON.gate_job` |
| `resources` (zprof `--metrics GpuTime`) | 1 frame, **per-PASS** GpuTime in ns | **one sample per arm**; parent's own frame-to-frame spread on this PASS is 1.36651 (fixed) vs 1.359166 (fb2) = **7.1 µs on 1.367 ms (0.52 %)** | `PASS-TIME-RANKING.json`, `resources/run/*/hardware-counters.json` |
| `ordinary` (warm 1 / reps 30 / feedback, 6 alternating groups) | 30 whole frames per arm | CI95 half-width **0.068 ms** on a 17.82 ms frame | `cards/n17-f20-body/RESULTS.json` |
| `native` (gtpin `--dump_isa`) | static ISA per kernel | exact instruction/message counts, no timing | `COMMON.native_job` |

Two consequences, and they drive the whole design:

1. **A one-transfer probe is unmeasurable.** One fragment transport = 8 SLM messages = 1.25 %
   of the 640-message runtime stream. Even under the most optimistic assumption (the dispatch
   is strictly message-throughput bound) that is 1.367 ms × 8/640 = **17 µs**: 4 × below the
   ordinary CI half-width and ~2.4 × the parent's own frame-to-frame spread on this PASS. A
   probe of that size would come back "INDISTINCT" for *any* true value below ~0.07 ms and
   would therefore answer nothing.
2. **The probe must amplify.** The card's thresholds have to be translated into the probe's
   own units, so the probe has to delete a *group* of transports and divide by that group
   size. Amplification only preserves the meaning of the number if the amplified ablation is
   still **single-variable**: it must remove transport and nothing else.

## 2. Delete-consumer vs global-direct-read — which one isolates the cost

The card offers two designs. Both were costed against the captured ISA before choosing.

### 2.1 "Delete one whole cross-SG transport (consumer included)" — confounded, ~1:1 with the signal

The card suggests stage 9 `O → pixel tail`. What that ablation actually removes (all counts
per warp from the captured ISA, `build/isa.json`):

| component | messages | other instructions |
|---|---:|---|
| 2 `coopMatStore` to `shX` (the O publication) | 16 | ~10 address adds |
| the pixel tail itself (`sg<2`, `boundary_project`, 16 × 8 fp32 mul-add, 4 `residual_out` stores) | 4 `send.ugm` | **≈420 instructions on 2 of 8 subgroups** |
| one barrier | 1 gateway | `sync.bar` |

So the "transport" is 16 of 640 runtime messages (2.5 %, ≤34 µs) while the consumer that has to
go with it is ≈2.2 % of the dispatch's instruction volume *and* destroys the frame. Signal and
confound are the same size, and the number that comes out cannot be split between them. On top
of that the frame gold fails, so the arm can never be gated or repeated as a candidate.

### 2.2 "Keep the numbers, read the same tile directly from global" — **message-neutral, and it adds DRAM**

This is the design that looks safest but is measurably not a cost probe. The captured ISA gives
the cost of *every* cooperative-matrix fragment transport in this kernel, and a **global**
fragment load or store is **not cheaper** than a shared one unless the tile happens to be a
contiguous column-major weight block:

| source-level transfer | captured messages per transfer | evidence (count / source sites) |
|---|---:|---|
| `coopMatLoad` from **shared**, RowMajor or ColumnMajor | **8** | `load.slm.d32x8t.a32` 304 / 38 sites |
| `coopMatStore` to **shared** | **8** | `store.slm.d32x8t.a32` 160 / 20 sites |
| `coopMatLoad` from **global** (weight B, 16×16, `LCOL`, stride 16 — contiguous) | **1** | `load.ugm.d32x8.a32` 40 / 40 sites |
| `coopMatLoad` from **global** (H fragment, stride 0) | **8** | `load.ugm.d32x8t.a32` 32 / 4 sites |
| `coopMatLoad` from **global** (C fragment, bias, stride 64) | **9** | `load.ugm.d32x16t.a32` 36 / 4 sites |
| `coopMatStore` to **global** (fragment, debug dump) | **8** | `store.ugm.d32x8t.a32` 96 / 12 sites |

(`build/isa.json → fragment_transfer_cost`, `all_checks_match = true`; each class divides
exactly by its independently enumerated, fully-unrolled source-site count.)

A cross-SG handoff re-pointed at a global buffer therefore costs **8 + 8 = 16 messages, exactly
what the SLM handoff costs**, while adding one 4 KiB tile per workgroup of real DRAM traffic
(16 393 WGs × 4 KiB = **64 MiB per dispatch**) and needing a coherence fence that the SLM form
does not need. Predicted outcome under the message hypothesis: **Δ ≈ 0 plus a DRAM penalty** —
i.e. the control cannot measure the marginal cost of a transport, it measures SLM-vs-UGM port
behaviour. It would also require a new global staging region, which the plan's binding sizes do
not have.

### 2.3 Chosen design: delete a whole *publication* and keep the consumer running

Single variable = **transport**; amplification = 16 transfers; consumer untouched.

> stage 1's M publication (`shArena`, 8 fragment stores) and stage 3's 8 reloads of exactly
> that publication are deleted. Stage 3's A operand becomes a register constant, so all 8 K-slice
> DPAS, the whole epilogue chain (norm → QKV → score → exp → den → PV → context → projection),
> the pixel tail, every barrier and every other message in the kernel stay exactly as they are.

Why this is the right amplification:

* it is **one named transport pair** (stage 1 → stage 3 is the single largest cross-SG round
  trip in the kernel: 16 of the 48 remaining cross-SG transports, 128 of 384 cross-SG messages);
* the consumer stays, so the Δ cannot contain deleted arithmetic (this is exactly the failure
  mode of §2.1);
* it is *upward* biased in a direction we want: it also removes the store→load RAW dependency
  and its address arithmetic, so the number is an **upper bound** on what message-count
  reduction alone can buy — and the card's decision rule is explicitly an *upper bound*
  ("剩余 48 次（384 条消息）上限");
* the values stay finite (stage 3's DPAS accumulate on the real seed), so `nan`/`inf` gates are
  expected to pass and no data-dependent-timing question arises.

Numerics differ by construction: **this is the probe's price**, stated once and accepted by the
card ("探针允许数值不等").

## 5. Timing arm and decision rule

Primary: `resources` (per-PASS `GpuTime` of pass 230) — the only instrument that reports the
dispatch in isolation; the parent's own spread on this PASS is 7.1 µs, and the predicted effect
is 20–40 × that. Secondary: `ordinary` (whole frame), see §6 for the gold caveat.

| measured Δ (probe − base, negative = probe faster) | per transfer (Δ/16) | what it implies for the remaining 48 transports (384 messages) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ −0.080 ms | ≤ 0.0050 ms | ≥ 0.24 ms | **redesign the cross-SG transports** (the 8→2 message layout is worth F20's share) |
| −0.080 … −0.016 ms | 0.0010 … 0.0050 | 0.048 … 0.24 ms | ambiguous: run the 8-transfer ladder (§7) with a self-consistent gold |
| ≥ −0.016 ms (or INDISTINCT) | ≤ 0.0010 ms | ≤ 0.048 ms of an 18.16 ms sum | **close the direction**; record that F20's 1.367 ms is not proportional to fragment-message count |

Interpretation is only allowed in combination with §4: **N1 must have landed**, otherwise the
result is triage A (implementation did not land), not evidence about transport cost.

## 6. Harness caveat the parent must know before submitting (gold)

The probe frame **cannot** equal either gold. In `templates/execute.ps1` a frame-gold mismatch
sets `status = FAILED_OR_STOPPED` **and `stop_reason`, which skips every later job in that
manifest**. Therefore:

* request the probe as its own stage (`N17B-PROBE`), arms ordered `base` then `probe`, so base is
  recorded before the stop;
* run `native` and `resources` first — both produce their data before the status is computed, so
  the probe's ISA capture and its per-PASS `GpuTime` are on disk even though the job is marked
  `FAILED_OR_STOPPED`;
* `ordinary` for the probe is only usable if the gold is made self-consistent: after the first
  probe run, take `frame_out_sha256` from `LUNA-DIRECT-CLOCK-RESULTS.json` and set it as
  `expected_frame_out` on the probe jobs in `spec-ordinary.json`. That is a **probe-local**
  gold (the probe's own frame), it does not touch `COMMON.FIXED_GOLD`/`FB2_GATE_GOLD`, and it
  must not be adopted into any card's gold afterwards. Without that override the probe gets
  exactly **one** ordinary sample and no CI — in which case the `resources` number is the
  result and the ordinary arm should be skipped, not faked.

## 7. Fallbacks (prepared, not built)

1. **N3 fires (dpas < 64)** — replace the constant A with the stage-3 seed the kernel already
   holds: keep `MAT_H probe_a` and set it from the `acc0` seed block, then
   `MAT_A a; a[0]=probe_a[0]; … a[3]=probe_a[3];` in the K loop. 4 extra instructions, no load,
   register-live only across the K loop, still not constant-foldable.
2. **Ladder** — build the 8-transfer variant (stage-3 A loads only, publication kept) and
   compare the two amplitudes: linear in messages ⇒ per-transfer slope is valid; strongly
   non-linear ⇒ the effect is the dependency/barrier, not the messages, and the "remaining 48"
   arithmetic in the card does not apply. Both variants are one-line edits of the same parent.
3. **Rescale** — if N2 shows the predicated stores are still issued, the ablation is
   −64 messages = 8 transfers and every threshold in §5 doubles.

## 8. Which deliverable may enter the chain

* **A (this probe): never.** It changes the frame by construction, it is not a candidate, and it
  carries no mechanism claim. Its only outputs are a per-message rate and an upper bound.
* **B (`candidate.spv` + `plan.txt` in this card, the Y round-trip removal): yes, as a
  candidate** — numerics identical (FP expression multiset 70/70 and NC 45/45 identical), gates
  and a same-batch ordinary win still required (see `ANALYSIS.md`, `NATIVE-PREDICTION.md`,
  `RESUME.txt`).
