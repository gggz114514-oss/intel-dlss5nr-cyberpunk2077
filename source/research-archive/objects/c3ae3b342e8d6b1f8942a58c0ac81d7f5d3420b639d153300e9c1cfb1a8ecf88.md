# F20 (ord 230) — ALU / address-chain audit (task B)

Card `n28-f20-barrier-alu`. Read-only analysis of the captured ISA and the parent SPIR-V; no GPU.

Inputs (hashes in `BUILD.json`):

* parent SPIR-V `215aca67…7f7e32` (29 `OpCooperativeMatrixLoadKHR`, of which **13 read a
  workgroup array**; the other 16 read `wb` / `fb` in global memory),
* ISA capture `CS_asm76902ef344609c8a_simd32_0000000000000027_38_orig.asm` (kernel hash
  `76902ef344609c8a`; instruction count **4754** per warp, scratch 0, `max_r` 127),
* tools: `tools/spv_sites.py` (site → shared block → index expression),
  `tools/addr_cones.py` (backward slice of the address operand of every load),
  `tools/depth_census.py` (longest dependent producer path for every instruction),
  `tools/isa_regions.py` (13 regions cut by the 12 `sync.bar`).

## 1. The 38 `coopMatLoad` sites, and what their address cones cost

The 13 shared-source SPIR-V loads unroll to **304 `load.slm.d32x8t`** in the ISA = 38 logical
fragment loads x 8 messages per fragment (`n17` established the 8-messages-per-fragment
constant independently). Multiplicities, straight from the source loops:

| SPIR-V site | stage | shared block | expression | copies | loads |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| #00 | 1 expand A(mA) | `shXP` | `shXP[mA*32 + k0]`, `mA = (sg/4 + 4r)*8` | 2 rounds x 2 ki | 4 |
| #01 | 1 expand A(mB) | `shXP` | `shXP[mB*32 + k0]`, `mB = mA + 16` | 2 x 2 | 4 |
| #02/#03 | 3 contract seed 0/1 | `shXP` | `shXP[m0*32 + {0,16}]`, `m0 = 8sg` | 1 | 2 |
| #04 | 3 contract A | `shArena` | `shArena[m0*128 + k0]`, 8 ki | 8 | 8 |
| #05/#06 | 6 score Q0/Q1 | `shArena` | `shArena[sg*8*32 + {0,16}]` | 1 | 2 |
| #07/#08 | 6 score K^T 0/1 | `shArena` | `shArena[2048 + n0*32 + k0]`, `n0 = 32j` | 2 x 2 each | 8 |
| #09/#10 | 8 context V0/V1 | `shArena` | `shArena[4096 + k0*32 + {0,16}]` | 4 each | 8 |
| #11/#12 | 9 projection seed 0/1 | `shY` | `shY[m0*32 + {0,16}]` | 1 | 2 |
| | | | | **total** | **38** |

**Address-cone depth** (longest dependent producer path ending at the message, counted in
instructions — `tools/addr-cones.py`, `tools/addr-cones.json`), over the 304 fragment loads:

| depth | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| loads | 8 | 120 | 1 | 21 | 101 | 31 | **22** |

* Maximum **8**, and 7 of the 13 sites never exceed 6.
* All but one of the depth-8 cones are in region 2 (stage 1 expand); the cone is
  `mov + and + or + shl + shr + add + add` — i.e. the row index `mA = (sg/4 + 4*round)*8` and
  the `k0` column offset, recomputed per fragment.
* The deepest cone in the whole kernel is **9**, and only four instructions reach it: two
  `load.ugm.d16u32` (the stage-0 coarse/skip pairs) and two `load.ugm.d32x8` (weight B). No
  `load.slm` fragment load exceeds 8.

So the card's premise ("the deepest chain feeds the 38 `coopMatLoad`") does not hold on this
capture: the fragment address cones are 2..8 instructions and most of them already terminate in
`add rX, rY, imm` (base + immediate), which is what `n04b` measured for F05 as well.

## 2. The three named shortenings, priced

| method (card's list) | legal? | what it would save | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(i) per-lane base precompute** — compute `mA*32` once per lane before the round loop, store it in a register, and let each of the 4 fragment loads use `base + imm` | legal (pure address arithmetic, no element changes) | the 8-instruction cone becomes 2 (one add + the send); 6 instructions x 8 stage-1 loads = **48 of 4754 = 1.0 %** of the static stream, 0 dynamic change (the loads are already issued) | **no budget.** 1 % of the address stream is not the ALUWR source, and L1 already measured this exact class twice: `n09` (F11, address folding, −158 SPIR-V ops) and `n04a` (F05 C64, −93 integer ops) both produced **zero** ISA change because IGC re-expands the folded form or emits base+imm anyway; `n04a` was −0.098 ms SLOWER_95 |
| **(ii) fold the 4 corners into an offset table** — the stage-0 k-loop's four `(dx, dy)` corners, and the `(mA, mB) x (k0, k16)` fragment corners | legal for stage 0 (no fragment load there); for the fragment loads the corners are already immediates (`+0`, `+16`, `+2048`, `+4096`) | 0 for the fragment loads (already folded by IGC); stage 0's corners are 2 shifts + 1 and per iteration and stage 0 has no `coopMatLoad` | **no budget.** Nothing to fold where the card points |
| **(iii) div/mod → shift** — `nr_tok_addr`'s `c/16`, `c%16`, `io_at`'s `t/16`, `px/4`, `py%4`, etc. | legal, and already done | the SPIR-V has 35 `OpUDiv` + 12 `OpUMod`, all by powers of two; the ISA has **no** `div`/`mod` opcodes at all — it has `shr` 12, `shl` 92, `and`/`or` 86 across the 13 regions | **nothing left.** IGC already lowered every constant division to shifts and masks |

Total theoretical saving if all three were applied: ~50 instructions of 4754 (**1.1 %**), none
of them on a dependency path that feeds a stall counter, on a kernel whose time is
`ALUWR 0.292 ms + BARRIER 0.224 ms` with `SENDWR` only 0.029 ms. Direction B has **no budget**.

## 3. Where the ALU dependency depth actually is

Global census of the longest dependent producer path ending at each instruction
(`tools/depth_census.py`, `tools/depth-census.json`, top 400):

| depth | region | instruction | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **33** | 11 (tail) | `add` feeding `send.ugm` at `0x10110`-ish | the tail's FP32 fold: 32 serial `mul`-then-`add` per output element, ending at the `residual_out` store |
| **32** | 11 | `add` | same chain, neighbouring element |
| **31** | 7 (stage 6/8) | `mov` | the P fragment hand-off: `for (d) a[d] = p_cache[ki][d]` copies plus the `pv_inv` multiplies and the H() narrowing that precede the context DPAS |
| **30** | 7 | `mov` | same chain, other component |
| ≤ 29 | 2, 3, 10 | fragment/seed address and staging moves | staging landing pads |

The two deepest chains are **not reachable for shortening**:

* the tail chain is a K-ordered FP32 accumulation. Contract §3 freezes the accumulation order
  and the `precise` qualifier forbids contraction, so no split-K, no tree, no FMA. Its *length*
  cannot change; only *who executes it* can — which is what the candidate does (4 chains per
  lane become 1 chain per lane, and 2 subgroups become 8; the chain per element is bit-identical).
* the region-7 chain is the FP16 rounding/repack line (acc → half → A operand). `n17b`/`n17c`
  measured the transport that produces it (Q and Y register hand-off, 64 messages) as
  INDISTINCT, and the rounding points themselves are frozen.

## 4. Conclusion for task B, and how it feeds task C

* Deepest chain feeding a `coopMatLoad`: **8 instructions** (stage-1 expand A), not a budget.
* Deepest chain in the kernel: **33** (tail FP32 fold), then **31** (region-7 repack) — both
  frozen by the numeric contract in *order*, but the tail's chain is frozen only in order, not
  in *ownership*.
* Therefore the larger budget is task A (one of five executed barriers) and it is realised by
  the same change that removes 3 of the 4 tail chains per lane — the candidate in
  `candidate.comp`, described in `BARRIER-AUDIT.md` §5 and `ANALYSIS.md`.

## 5. What would reopen B

If a future capture shows a fragment address cone > 12 instructions, or if `XVE_STALL_ALUWR`
survives the tail change and the ISA shows the address arithmetic clustered around the
`send.slm` issue slots, then direction (i) becomes worth a card. The measurement that would
decide it: per-region instruction mix plus the ALUWR counter on a candidate whose only change is
the hoisted per-lane base.
