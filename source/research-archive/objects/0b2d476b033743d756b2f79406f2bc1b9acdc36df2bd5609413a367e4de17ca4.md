# R02 segment contract 2/4 — QK (drafted, NOT implemented)

Sites: 16 of the parent's 64 (word offsets 19664…22935, result ids 3566…4252, `main`);
source lines 818–1019. Proof of correspondence: `evidence/SITE-SOURCE-MAP.json` ordinals 24–39.
Still open in the candidate module: `spv/r02_body1_qkv_i8.spv` carries all 16 unchanged
(`evidence/SITES-candidate.json`, segment `QK`).

## 1. Operands

| | parent (unchanged today) | proposed INT8 |
|---|---|---|
| A | `NRH_A` FP16 8×16, **RowMajor**, stride 32, from shared `shK` at `p*256 + {0,16}`, `p<8` | `I8A` int8 8×32 K, ColumnMajor stride 32: keys 8p..8p+7 × **all 32 dims** in one step |
| B | `NRH_B` FP16 16×16 from the register handoff `qb0 = join(q0,q1)` (dims 0..15), `qb1 = join(q2,q3)` (dims 16..31) | `I8B` int8 32×16: `Q^T[dim, query]`, quantised after the norm multiply |
| C | `NRH_C` f32 8×16, zero; two K16 steps chained | `I8C` int32 8×16, zero; **one** K32 step |
| out | `sc` → `+bias` (FP32, `precise`) → `nrh_narrow` → `p0..p7` half → `nr_exp_h2` | same FP32 bias and same narrow point, from the INT8 epilogue |

Owner is unchanged: this segment owns the **transposed** score `S^T[key, query]`, and the
A operand is K (keys), the B operand is Q (queries) — the transpose is a property of the
operand roles, not of a separate store, so the INT8 form must keep A = K and B = Q^T.

MMA count: 16 → 8 (two FP16 K16 steps become one INT8 K32 step per key block).
Inputs do **not** have to be re-laid-out: dim = 32 = exactly one K32.

## 2. Scale contract (proposed)

| factor | source | exists? |
|---|---|---|
| `sK[key]` per-key (per token) | dynamic, from the post-norm K fragment (amax over the 32 dims) | **no** — needs a cross-lane reduction |
| `sQ[query]` per-query (per token) | dynamic, from the post-norm Q fragment × `scalar0` (amax over 32 dims) | **no** |
| zero point | symmetric, none | — |

Because both sides are dynamic, the dequant is a **rank-1 outer product**
`acc[key,query] * sK[key] * sQ[query]`: two multiplies per element, and the per-channel
folding trick used by QKV/output does **not** apply. That is the connection cost of this
segment and the reason it is drafted rather than implemented.

## 3. Rounding points

1. K/Q quantization: `roundEven` + clamp ±127 **after** the per-token norm and (for Q) after
   `scalar0`, i.e. exactly on the values the parent writes to `shK` / feeds as B;
2. integer accumulation exact;
3. dequant (2 multiplies) into FP32;
4. `+ bias` in FP32 with `precise` (unchanged, stays outside the INT8 arm);
5. one narrow to half, then the existing packed `nr_exp_h2`.

## 4. Residual / seed

None in this segment: `sc` starts at zero and the bias is added after the accumulator
(the parent's `precise float nr_score_biased_*` shape is kept).

## 5. Named gaps (must close before implementation)

1. **Per-head / per-token consumption contract.** The 32 dims of one token live in 4
   fragments whose lanes hold 4 values each; an amax over a token's 32 dims is a
   cross-lane reduction across the 16-lane parity group (`nrh_coord_CH`/`nrh_feature_parity`
   already encode the measured map). No such reduction exists in the parent; writing one is
   new work with its own numeric contract.
2. **Score error through the softmax.** The QK output feeds `exp`, a denominator tree and a
   reciprocal; the INT8 error is multiplied by the score magnitude *before* the exponential.
   A per-key/per-query scale bound has to be derived, not assumed.
3. **No capture.** There is no captured post-norm Q/K tile for this arm, so even the local
   oracle that exists for QKV cannot be produced yet.
4. **P must stay in half.** The parent forms P in registers and hands it to PV through
   `nrh_join_rows_to_B`; if QK goes INT8, P is still the FP16 boundary — P's quantization is
   the PV segment's business, and doing both at once would mix two changes.
