## 1. What is fixed here

Six axis-aligned rectangles, determined **once** on frame **34189** at **1280x720**, then applied
unchanged to every frame and to **both** INT8 arms (XE and the older c64). No per-frame, per-arm
or per-image ROI adaptation is performed anywhere in `tools/metrics.py`.

Coordinate convention: half-open pixel ranges `[x0, x1) x [y0, y1)`, origin at the top-left
corner, x to the right, y downwards, in the same 1280x720 frame as the float buffer's plane 0.

## 2. Source image and why these coordinates transfer to the float data

- `Q/visual-comparison/DISPLAY-CONTRACT.json` states: frame 34189, source = application RGB
  `[0,:,:,:3]` float32, mapping = identical `clip(0,1)`, `round(value*255)`, no independent
  exposure / tone mapping / gamma.
- Independent check (`tools/verify_display.py`): `Q/visual-comparison/fp16.png` equals
  `round(clip(network-output.bin plane0, 0, 1) * 255)` on **2,764,794 of 2,764,800** components;
  the 6 differing components differ by exactly 1 LSB (tie rounding only). So coordinates read off
  the published PNG address the same pixels as the float reference buffer.
- The candidate arm is *not* used for ROI placement, so the mask cannot be biased toward either arm.

## 5. What is missing (why this is not a per-surface mask)

- No segmentation, object-id, depth or albedo buffer was captured, so the ROIs are rectangles, not
  per-pixel surface masks; a rectangle can mix a surface with its background.
- No neutral reference patch exists in all three frames (concrete in this scene carries blue
  ambient and orange paint), so `neutral-wall` is only neutral *on 34189* and cannot serve as a
  white-balance anchor.
- The output colour space / transfer function of `frame_out` is unverified, so ROI numbers are raw
  RGB differences; no Lab, DeltaE or luminance in cd/m2 is claimed.
- The ROI set was placed by visual inspection of the published 1280x720 PNG plus 32x32 block
  statistics of the FP16 float buffer; there is no independent ground-truth annotation to check it
  against, and no second annotator.
- Motion frames would need per-frame feature tracking to keep the same surface; that is
  deliberately **not** done here because the card requires one fixed mask.
