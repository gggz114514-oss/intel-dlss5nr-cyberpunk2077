## Direct contradiction of previous report

The original captured baseline native contains `mad (32|M0) ... :hf` at PC 0x8190, inside norm before math.rsqt at 0x8270. The claim that the baseline norm is pure mul+add is false. PARENT-C7-NATIVE.json records the binary hash and instruction anchors.

The operand chain is column15 loaded as the upper half of the last dword from the column8..15 block, squared and rounded into r63. Column31 is loaded by the deferred d16 send at 0x8020 (row address +62 bytes), moved to r10, then consumed twice by mad. The resulting branch is H(v31*v31 + H(v15*v15)), feeding s[7]. This is different from globally prohibiting square/add contraction. No inference of wait cycles is made from SWSB.
