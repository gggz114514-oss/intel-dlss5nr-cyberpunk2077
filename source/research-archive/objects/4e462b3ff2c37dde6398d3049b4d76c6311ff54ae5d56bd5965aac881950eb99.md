## 1. Mapping — what each (lane, component) is, and what the consumer needs

### 1a. Structure of the dispatch (exact, from the source)

```
WG = 128 lanes = 4 subgroups, sg = lane_id/32, lane = lane_id%32, hi = lane>>4
ax = 2*gx + (sg & 1)          ay = 2*gy + (sg >> 1)          (gx,gy = gl_WorkGroupID)
ch = 2*(lane & 15)            slot_j = 2*j + hi,  j = 0..7
px_j = 4*ax + (slot_j & 3)    py_j = 4*ay + (slot_j >> 2)   (the skip-image pixel)
```
So one WG covers an 8×8 pixel block (four 4×4 tiles), and the 8 blend calls of one
lane cover the 8 slots `2j + hi`.

### 1b. Enumerated (lane, component) → consumer table

`nr_blend_pair(slot_j, ch)` returns `f16vec2 value` = the blended pair for
**channels `{ch, ch+1}` at pixel `(px_j, py_j)`**. Each of the 8 results is packed
into one `uint` and read back by two subgroup shuffles. The 16 component
assignments per lane are:

| consumer component | source call `j` | shuffle lane `sl` | selected half |
|---|---|---|---|
| `nx00[k]`, k=0..3 | `k` | `16*hi + 0 + ((lane&15)>>1)` | `x` if `lane` even else `y` |
| `nx01[k]`, k=0..3 | `k` | `16*hi + 8 + ((lane&15)>>1)` | `x` if `lane` even else `y` |
| `nx10[k]`, k=0..3 | `4+k` | `16*hi + 0 + ((lane&15)>>1)` | `x` if `lane` even else `y` |
| `nx11[k]`, k=0..3 | `4+k` | `16*hi + 8 + ((lane&15)>>1)` | `x` if `lane` even else `y` |

* **Coverage is exact**: `4` matrices × `4` components = 16 = `8` calls × `2`
  components. Every `(j, half)` is consumed by exactly one `(component, lane)`
  slot; no slot is read twice, none is left unread. (The parent's own `PROOF`
  establishes the same for `nrh_to_A`/`nrh_coord_*`; the candidate does not touch
  any of that.)
* **Producer side localisation**: a consumer lane `L` with `u = L&15` reads
  producer lane `L' = 16*(L>>4) + off + (u>>1)`, `off ∈ {0,8}`, whose
  `ch = 2*(L'&15)`, and takes half `x` when `L` is even, `y` when odd. Hence the
  channel seen by `(L, off)` is `2*(L'&15) + (L&1)`, and over all 32 lanes and
  both `off` values the 32 channels are each covered once. Nothing about this
  changes in the candidate: the shuffles, the pack/unpack and the select are
  byte-identical.
* **Token coverage**: with `sg` fixed, `(ax, ay)` is fixed, and `slot_j` walks
  0..15 as `(j, hi)` walk `0..7 × 0..1`, i.e. the 16 `(sx,sy)` of the 4×4 tile
  group. The parent's `tok_base`/`tok_oob_u` path (the *store* side) is
  untouched, so the "last partial tile" question is answered by the parent's
  proof and remains valid.

### 1c. What the candidate changes in this map

**Nothing.** The producer addresses `sa` and the `shK` index are recomputed with
a different *expression*, but `proof-address.json` shows they evaluate to the
identical integers for all 753 664 reachable combinations, and the two gain
pairs are read from the identical indices (they do not depend on `j` at all).
The change is: (i) one address base per lane instead of eight div/mod chains,
(ii) one read of each gain pair instead of eight.

## 2. Which real work on the normal path is deleted

Only these, and all of them are integer/load work:

| # | work | per lane | evidence |
|---|---|---:|---|
| R1 | `sy = slot/4`, `sx = slot%4` (8 call sites) | 8 `shr` + 8 `and` | ISA shows `shl/shr/and` triples at the blend sites |
| R2 | `py/4`, `px/4` (exact by construction) | 8 `shr` | SPIR-V `OpUDiv 15 -> 9`, `OpShiftRightArithmetic 79 -> 73` |
| R3 | `py%4`, `px%4` | 8 `and` | SPIR-V `OpUMod 7 -> 2` |
| R4 | `(py/4)*(W/4)`, `(px/4)*16`, `4*(py%4)` and friends | ~19 `mul` | SPIR-V `OpIMul 188 -> 175` |
| R5 | `t/16`, `t%16` | 8 `shr` + 8 `and` | same as R2/R3 |
| R6 | **duplicate `skip_gain` gather** | 7 of 8 `send.ugm.d16u32` | ISA: 8 identical `pw[pc.p5.z+ch], +ch+1` gathers |
| R7 | **duplicate `proj_gain` gather** (under `pc.p5.w==1u`) | 7 of 8 gathers | ISA: 8 gather sites in the projection-gain spans |
| R8 | dead source function `nrf2_io_at0` (post-change) | — | SPIR-V `OpFunction 14 -> 13` |

**Not deleted, deliberately** (and listed so the ledger cannot be misread):

* `dpas.8x8` 129 — untouched (3.1 % of the code, already minimal).
* 441 `f32->f16` narrowing `mov` — these *are* the half rounding points (Q4
  "half 舍入点不变"); removing any of them would change numerics.
* 3 `barrier()` / `sync.bar` + `signal barrier` + `fence.slm`, the SLM `shK`/`shV`
  round trip, the 8 `sk[sa]` reads, the 8 stride-0 scale broadcasts,
  129 DPAS, 64 B-fragment weight loads.
* the 384 debug `store.ugm.d32x8t` and the 4 `if(!tok_oob_u)` output stores —
  the candidate does not touch the `debug` or the `tok_oob` guards at all.
* 184 `sync.nop` (dependency scoreboard waits) — compiler schedule products.

## 3. Overlap — what was independent, and what protects the feed

Legend: **P** producer, **C** last consumer, **O** overrider. RAW/WAR/WAW are for
the *A operand*, the *weight B fragments*, the *accumulators*, the *norm/den
reduction*, and the *blend inputs*.

| value | P | C | O | hazard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A fragment `xa00…xa11` (from `nx** × scale`) | blend + `nrh_to_A` | 8 `coopMatMulAdd` (E expand) | next token tile (new WG) | RAW (8 readers), WAR vs the following `y` reuse — unchanged |
| weight `B` tiles from `wb` | `coopMatLoad` (48 UGM) | 1 `coopMatMulAdd` each | not overwritten (read-only buffer) | RAW, pure read — unchanged |
| accumulators `ec0/ec1`, `ny0*`, `z00..z11`, `sc0/sc1`, `tc**`, `oc**` | `coopMatMulAdd` chains | `nrh_narrow` | overwritten by the next K chunk | RAW → **the candidate keeps every `NRH_C` name, order and `precise` qualifier, so the accumulator chain and its overlap are untouched** |
| `q**`/`k**` norm | `full_norm` (per component, 8 calls) | the `k→shK` SLM store and the `q` A-fragment | `shK` overwritten by the next WG | RAW + WAR on `shK`; the 3 `barrier()` order is unchanged |
| residual/softmax `d` | 4 shuffle-XOR steps, twice per `p` group | `inv` then `p*inv` | next `p` group | RAW; the reduction tree (8 → 4 → 2 → 1) is untouched |
| **blend pair** | `nr_blend_pair` (8 calls) | `packFloat2x16` + 2 shuffles | — | **the only value whose *production* changed**: 8 address chains → 1, 16 gathers → 2. The produced *values* and their consumers are identical |
| `skip_gain` / `proj_gain` | 1 gather each (new) | the 8 `skip_v`/`main_scaled` products | — | new: WAR against nothing (read-only), RAW to 8 consumers. Previously each inlined copy produced and consumed its own copy; hoisting merges 8 identical producers into 1, which is a pure CSE *at the source level* |

The goal of the card was "delete work and protect the feed"; nothing was
serialised: the hoisted values sit in the same region (above the first blend
call, below the `tok_oob_u` broadcast), no barrier moved, and no new dependency
edge crosses a barrier or a DPAS. The `precise` qualifiers on
`main_scaled`/`skip_v`/`value` are kept so the rounding order of the three FP
operations is unchanged.

## 5. Does it really land in native (prediction + falsification criteria)

**Answered by measurement.** The main agent's gates and native arms for this card
have already run; I read them back offline (`ISA-ACCOUNT.json`, and
`NATIVE-PREDICTION.md` §B has the full table). Summary:

| decisive observable | base | cand | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `send.ugm.d16u32` gathers | 34 | **20** | **landed** (prediction exact) |
| `send.ugm` | 604 | **590** | landed |
| `add3` / `shl` / `mul` / `or` | 12 / 118 / 253 / 49 | **7 / 100 / 245 / 39** | the div/mod chains are gone |
| instructions | 5384 | **5276 (−2.01 %)** | landed, but 7× smaller than predicted |
| `dpas.8x8` / `sync.bar` / scratch / `math.rsqt` / `math.inv` | 129 / 3 / 0 / 16 / 8 | **identical** | invariants hold |
| `sync.nop` | 184 | **204 (+10.9 %)** | **B-class side effect** |
| golds fixed `1755e9c2…` / fb2 `9c3e511e…`, nan/inf 0 | — | identical | no E |

So: **not A** (the mechanism is in the ISA), **B** (it brought a measurable
scoreboard cost), and E is excluded. The three falsifiers below were the
pre-registered criteria and are kept for the record:

1. `send.ugm.d16u32` count 34 → 20. Threshold ≥ 30 identical ⇒ **A**.
2. The eight blend address chains are gone: `add3 12 → 7`.
3. Invariants `dpas 129`, `sync.bar 3`, `scratch 0`, golds unchanged.

If (1) and (2) hold, the change is implemented; then, and only then, look at
`GpuTime`.

## 6. Is it faster overall — what this card must measure

**What to measure.** Single-PASS scope, so the isolated per-PASS GpuTime is the
primary arm (`zprof --metrics GpuTime`, `--pool --warm 0 --reps 1`), plus the
ordinary whole-plan arm (`--pool --warm 1 --reps 30 --feedback`) because the
PASS only exists as part of the 232-PASS plan.

**Arms.** `base` = chain head `restore-n10-f10-next-20261027/plan.txt`
(`719f100b…`); `cand` = this card's `plan.txt` (`6a665698…`), identical except
`PASS 147`. Two-arm alternating, 6 alternating blocks of ordinary, plus the
fixed/fb2 gates for correctness.

**Attribution.** The plan differs in exactly one line (`replaced == 1`), so any
`GpuTime` delta is attributable to this PASS; the whole-plan ordinary delta must
be read as "this PASS's share", not as a new mechanism class, and must not be
added to the campaign's cumulative millisecond accounting without the same-batch
A-arm.

**Honest bound (corrected after the capture).** The ISA says the kernel is 3.1 %
DPAS and ~70 % address/mov: the blend cluster is 8 inlined call sites of a
helper whose per-call chain shrank 158 → 51 SPIR-V instructions. I predicted
−9…−15 % of the kernel; the **measured** delta is **−108 instructions
(−2.01 %)**, because IGC had already shared most of the helper and only the
div/mod chain plus the duplicated loads were really replicated. What is left on
the critical path is unchanged: 129 DPAS and the 48 weight block loads.
Expect **≤1 % of the PASS (≤0.0026 ms)** and a real chance of **D (INDISTINCT)**
at plan scope; the pre-registered counter-signal is `sync.nop 184 → 204`, i.e.
the side effect is at least as large as the win, which is why the follow-up in
`RESUME.txt` §6(1) splits the two sub-changes and measures them separately.
