## Controls and limits

Old-runner minus new-runner/off: mean0.044746ms,CI[-0.274889,+0.364381]. New-runner/off minus selected: mean0.096236ms,CI[-0.599270,+0.791742]. These intervals are too wide to demonstrate equivalence or negligible timestamp perturbation. Do not claim profiling is free, do not accept the candidate from this local result, and do not treat the profiled whole-frame mean as a new ordinary result.

Selected interval includes first-use clears,dispatch and its existing post-dispatch barrier. No shader instrumentation,no235-timestamp pass-wide profiling. It cannot say norm/SLM/synchronization individually consumed particular milliseconds.

## Dependency still unresolved

Score requires both normalized Q and all required K. Retaining Q eliminates some transport but not the original raw norm input publication,coefficient dependency or cross-subgroup normalized-K barrier. Therefore Q being earlier is insufficient if K is still late. This is a verified structural gate, not a proven exposed runtime bottleneck. Likewise complete den still precedes normalized P→PV. No unique waiting chain or '15ms outside XMX' decomposition is claimed.

## Decision

Preserve score-owner as accepted baseline. Pause new shader experiments. The next measurement needs tighter old/new and timestamp perturbation controls, preferably avoiding long separate-process blocks while preserving independent feedback state. Only then use targeted internal-dependency diagnostics to choose a code change. Do not repeat the same broad ordinary batches or select favorable samples.
