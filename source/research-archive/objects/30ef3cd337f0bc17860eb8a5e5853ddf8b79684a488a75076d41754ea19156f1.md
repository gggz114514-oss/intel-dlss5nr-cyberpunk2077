## 0. What R02 had to do, and what this card did

R02 covers the seven C32 body passes `block1/2/3/4/67/68/69_swin_body_1`. All seven run the
*same* module `c32_body1_i8.spv`, 64 static FP16 MMA each (448 static FP16 matrix steps for the
card). The expand/contract pair is already INT8; the remaining work is split by the GUIDE into
**QKV, QK, PV, output**.

Delivered here:

* the 64 sites enumerated from the module and attributed to the four segments
  (24 / 16 / 16 / 8), each tied to its source line by an address identity proof;
* four INT8 contracts (`contracts/QKV.md`, `QK.md`, `PV.md`, `OUTPUT.md`);
* **one segment implemented**: QKV. New module `spv/r02_body1_qkv_i8.spv`
  sha256 `c72a1a238abf0d53d735cc8c0d80f3c58ec496bbc2da95f85ee9e1cb15de9d0b` (130884 B);
  24 FP16 MMA → 12 INT8 MMA; parent replayed byte-identically; `spv_validate_cpu result=0`;
  word-level diff fully attributed with one named residual;
* offline QKV weight packs for **all seven** blocks and a **block1** scale table, plus a
  segment-local integer oracle run on a real captured activation tile;
* the remaining FP16 inventory re-enumerated on the new module (40 sites: QK 16, PV 16,
  output 8) with the new module's own word offsets and result ids.

## 3. This arm's INT8 reference (closure rule 3)

`evidence/QKV-PACK-ORACLE.json`. Generated by `tools/qkv_pack_oracle.py` (this card, CPU only).

* **What it is**: a segment-local integer oracle. It takes the FP16 Q/K/V weight planes out of
  the pass's own bound half-weight buffer through the parent shader's literal address
  expression, quantises them per output channel (`s[n] = amax_k|W[n,k]|/127`, RNE, clamp ±127),
  packs the twelve I8A K32 tiles through the kernel's own tile addressing (round-trip decoded
  and compared element for element), and runs both arms in numpy on a real activation tile:
  FP16 reference (half × half, float32 accumulation, narrow to half) versus the INT8 arm
  (int32 accumulation, one dequant multiply per output channel, one narrow).
* **Activation source**: `run/int8-coverage-v2-20261020/C/c32-i8-diag-b1-eact-001/diag_b1_debug.bin`
  — a debug dump produced by the deployed parent SPV: the lock receipt
  `run/codex-sol-int8-long-008-20261019/exports/c32-i8-diag-b1-eact-001.lock-receipt.json`
  pins the argv to `plan-c32-i8-diag-b1-eact.txt`, whose `PASS 1` binds
  `c32_body1_i8.spv` (sha256 above) with `p0.z=1` (debug). The tile is read at
  `dbg[8192 + sg*512 + t*32 + c]`, exactly where the parent stores its contract output Y.
* **Non-zero**: yes. activation absmax 113.25, `invY` = 1.1214, saturation 0.049 % of 2048
  codes, reference output absmax 47.2 / 70.8 / 60.7 for Q/K/V.
* **Passed?** As a *segment-level numeric reference*, yes — the INT8 arm reproduces the FP16
  arm with

  | proj | rel L2 | RMS | max abs | SNR |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Q | 1.895 % | 0.278 | 0.973 | 34.45 dB |
  | K | 1.479 % | 0.276 | 1.094 | 36.60 dB |
  | V | 1.634 % | 0.281 | 0.910 | 35.74 dB |

  and the int32 accumulation is bit-identical to an int64 accumulation (exactness check).
* **What it is not**: not a GPU gate, not a frame-level verdict, not a quality/PSNR result. The
  FP16 arm is a float32 accumulation *reference*, not a hardware replay, so the numbers are the
  quantization error of this arm — they do not certify the module on the device.
* **Where it is weak**: the activation scale is per tensor over one 16-token × 32-channel tile
  from one frame. That is the dominant error term (a single 113.25 outlier costs ~7 bits); a
  per-token scale is the stage-2 improvement and needs a cross-lane amax that does not exist in
  the parent yet.

  `fsc[260]` load per component;

ledger grids, 4 active subgroups of 32 lanes each: block1 84×48 = 4032, block2 85×49 = 4165,
block3 85×48 = 4080, block4 84×49 = 4116, block67 85×49, block68 85×48, block69 84×49.

* per subgroup instance: +512 int8 stores, +2 barrier instructions (subgroup scope),
  −24 FP16 MMA, +12 INT8 MMA, +12 I8A loads, +1 I8B load, +48 dequant multiplies;
* per block per frame: +8.26 M (block1) to +8.53 M (block2/67) int8 stores,
  −387 072 to −399 840 FP16 MMA, +193 536 to +199 920 INT8 MMA,
  +4032 to +4165 barrier instruction pairs;

## 6. What changed, in memory-access and lifecycle terms

* **Deleted**: 24 FP16 weight loads from `wb` (binding 0), 16 shuffles + pack/unpack of the two
  FP16 B handoffs, 24 FP16 MMA.
* **Added**: 12 I8A weight loads from `w8` (binding 5) in the appended region
  `w8[32768..45056)`, 12 INT8 MMA, 2048 B declared shared `sY8` (4 × 512 B, one slot per
  subgroup, written and read by the same subgroup only), 2 barriers per workgroup.
* **Lifetime**: `y0..y3` (the contract row) are still narrowed and still feed the output
  residual seed `H(Y*ars)`; they now additionally feed `sY8` inside the same block, so their
  live range does not extend past the quantization. `shK`/`shV`/`q*/k*/v*` lifetimes and the
  cross-SG `barrier()` publication are untouched.
* **Not changed**: expand/contract math and K order, the four `fsc` entries 0..163, the contract
  seed, the output residual, norm/score/bias/exp/denominator/reciprocal, PV, the output
  projection, the image stores and their OOB rule, the grid, the descriptors, the push
  constants, and the fact that this is **one fused kernel** (no split into separate quant
  kernels).

## 7. Named blockers and next steps (closure rule 6)

1. **QK** (`contracts/QK.md`): both operands are dynamic activations; the dequant becomes a
   rank-1 outer product `sK[key]·sQ[query]`, and the per-token amax over 32 dims is a cross-lane
   reduction the parent does not have. Also needs a captured post-norm Q/K tile. 16 → 8 MMA.
2. **PV** (`contracts/PV.md`): P is the second dynamic end (per-query amax over 64 keys after
   the reciprocal); V ownership must be decided (dequantise once, or share the QKV per-channel
   factor). 16 → 8 MMA. Deliberately not done together with QK.
3. **output** (`contracts/OUTPUT.md`): the INT8 epilogue reference is now defined
   (`half(acc·sC·sWo + seed)`, one narrow, residual `H(Y*ars)` unchanged) because an INT32
   accumulator cannot inherit the FP32 seed; what is missing is the WO weight pack
   (+4096 B/block), the context activation scale and any capture. 8 → 4 MMA.
4. **`invY` calibration for blocks 2,3,4,67,68,69**: their passes still run the parent module.
   Needs one captured Y tile per block (the diag plan mechanism already exists) and a
   calibration decision (per tensor vs per token).
5. **`NoContraction` decoration quirk** on the four contract-seed multiplies: numerically inert
   (proved: no `OpFAdd` consumer) but not explained. Next step is either a compiler-flag
   experiment or accepting it under a GPU numeric gate.
6. **Plan/pool growth**: `w8` grows 32768 → 45056 B and `fsc` 656 → 1044 B per switched block
   (+12288 +388 B × 7 if all blocks are switched). Pool arithmetic was validated
   (`validate_pool_upload.py PASS`) but its resident-set effect is not measured.
7. **No GPU**: gate/ordinary/quality are un-run. `run/r02-c32-body/jobs.json` lists 5 gate jobs
   (parent fixed/fb2, candidate fixed/fb2, candidate diag) and 6 ordinary jobs (3 process
   pairs × 30 samples). The jobs definition deliberately does **not** assert a numeric
   tolerance between the candidate and the parent frame: for this card byte equality is only
   the *self-determinism* test between two runs of the same candidate plan, and the
   frame-vs-parent delta is recorded for the coordinating entry to judge against the named
   segment reference.
