WHAT WAS PROVEN (not assumed)
  1. FP16 weight pack layout for b23: W1/W2/W3 16x16 blocked, Wc plain row-major [N][K],
     rs at 786432, decoded from push p2/p3 and reproduced the teacher F bit-exactly for
     frame 43654 (equal fraction 1.0) and to 8.3e-5 relL2 for 34189.
  2. int8 pack layout: l0 536584 B in 7 regions, l1 264192 B, l1 pack weight matrix is
     ROW-MAJOR (a 16x16-block decode of it is wrong and was rejected by measurement).
  3. The l0 stage order and grouping: 8 groups, stage1 512x512 full-K then per-group
     64->256, half activation, per-group 256->64, contract 512x512 + diagonal seed X*rs.
  4. The old arm's real local error, and that its worst frames are 3-4x worse than 34189
     only because the single-frame scale choice does not transfer.

WHY THE GAIN IS WHAT IT IS
  Y is dominated by the residual seed (|contract term| / |Y| = 0.41-0.69%), so dY = dA and
  the Y objective is exactly the propagated error. The old arm's dominant loss was NOT the
  X scale: it was (a) a stage-1 scale that clipped 17% of the activation input tail on two
  frames, (b) a contract-input step sized from absmax(F) while F is 95% exact zeros with a
  90-190x absmax/rms ratio, and (c) no per-channel X allocation (channel absmax spans
  5.2..35.0). Fixing the three gave 3.2x.

NEGATIVE RESULTS KEPT
  * per-input-channel diagonal on the contract input alone (F equalisation) makes Y WORSE
    (1.756e-03 vs 1.031e-03 scalar-only) because Wc column norms are already near-uniform

  * one-shot equalisation without re-tuning the scalars also looks worse; the fair test

  * F relL2 is a misleading local metric (cancellation-dominated); do not rank arms by it.
