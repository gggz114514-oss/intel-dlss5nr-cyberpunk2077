# Named gaps — what is not implemented on R20 / R21, and what it needs

Card `r20-r21-tail`. Every item below is a **named, still-open** coverage gap. None of them is an
"exception", and none of them may be reported as whole-network completion.

Status vocabulary: `DONE` = implemented in this card; `CONTRACT` = the shape is known and the
missing piece is named; `NOT_STARTED` = no contract yet.
