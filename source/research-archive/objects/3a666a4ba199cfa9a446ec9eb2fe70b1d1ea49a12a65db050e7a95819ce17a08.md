# R02 segment contract 3/4 — PV (drafted, NOT implemented)

Sites: 16 of the parent's 64 (word offsets 25885…26509, result ids 4872…5001, `main`);
source lines 1232–1322. Proof: `evidence/SITE-SOURCE-MAP.json` ordinals 40–55.
Still open in the candidate module: all 16 unchanged (segment `PV`).

## 1. Operands

| | parent (unchanged today) | proposed INT8 |
|---|---|---|
| A | `NRH_A` FP16 8×16, **ColumnMajor**, stride 32, from shared `shV` at `q*512 + {0,8,16,24}`, `q<4` | `I8A` int8 8×32: 8 output channels × **32 keys per step**, two steps for the 64 keys |
| B | `NRH_B` FP16 from `pb = join_rows_to_B(p_even, p_odd)`, 4 groups covering the 64 keys | `I8B` int8 32×16: `P[key, query]`, quantised per query after the reciprocal |
| C | `NRH_C` f32 8×16 (`tc0..tc3`), zero, chained over 4 key groups (K=64) | `I8C` int32 8×16, zero, 2 × K32 |
| out | `ctx0..ctx3` = 32 channels × 16 tokens (half), then `tb0/tb1 = join(ctx0,ctx1)/(ctx2,ctx3)` | identical fragments from the INT8 epilogue |

MMA count: 16 → 8. K = 64 keys, so the K32 form needs exactly two steps per accumulator —
the 4 FP16 K16 groups merge pairwise, no channel re-layout.

## 2. Scale contract (proposed)

| factor | source | exists? |
|---|---|---|
| `sP[query]` per-query | dynamic, from the **normalised** P (the parent already multiplies every entry by the per-token `inv` before PV) | **no** |
| `sV[o]` per output channel | static per tensor **if** V is dequantised once by the QKV segment; dynamic per token if V is consumed in codes directly | **no** |

Dequant is again a rank-1 outer product `acc[o,q] * sV[o] * sP[q]`, unless V is handed over
in codes with the QKV segment's own per-channel factor, in which case only `sP[q]` remains
(one multiply per element). Both variants must be named in the pack; the second is the
stage-2 register-direct form and is *not* what this draft assumes.

## 3. Rounding points

1. P quantization: after the reciprocal multiply (`p = p * r`), `roundEven`, clamp ±127;
2. V quantization: at its production point (the QKV epilogue) or on the half V right before
   `shV`, one of the two, never both;
3. integer accumulation exact; dequant FP32; **one** narrow per fragment (`ctx*`), same place
   as the parent.

## 4. Residual / seed

None: `tc*` starts at zero. The denominator and reciprocal stay in half with their current
order (`den` tree → parity peer → `1/max(sum, 6.103515625e-05)`), and the reciprocal is
applied to P **before** quantization, so no probability rescaling is introduced.

## 5. Named gaps

1. **P is the second dynamic end.** PV's B is a probability matrix that only exists in
   registers; its per-query amax is a cross-lane reduction over the 64 keys that a token owns,
   which is 4 fragments per query column — no such reduction exists in the parent.
2. **Two dynamic ends at once.** Doing QK and PV together would put two new scale reductions
   in one change; the GUIDE requires one segment at a time, so PV is drafted after QK.
3. **No capture** of post-reciprocal P for this arm.
4. **V ownership.** V is produced by the QKV segment, which is now INT8: the cheapest correct
   stage-1 handoff is to dequantise V once (as today) and re-quantise for PV, which costs one
   extra conversion pass; consuming V in codes directly is the stage-2 item and needs the
   QKV per-channel factor to be shared, i.e. an explicit cross-segment identity.
