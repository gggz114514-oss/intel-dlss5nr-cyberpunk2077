### ② Did the `-0.247 ms` (`-0.261 ms` in `GAME-COMPARISON.json`) CPU reduction in `composite_submit_ms` propagate to final completion (`total_ms`)?

- **No (`Δ total_ms p50 = -0.0013 ms`, `mean = +0.0189 ms`)**, and the paired per-frame CPU + GPU measurements prove the exact mechanism:
  - In same-process `B1 → C1 → B2 → C2` paired windows (`719` post-settling frames on Arm B, `717` on Arm C):
    - `composite_submit_cpu_ms` decreases by **`-0.247096 ms` mean / `-0.237700 ms` p50** (`0.292562 ms → 0.045466 ms`, `-84.5%`).
    - `tail_wait_cpu_ms` (`WaitForSingleObject(completion)`) increases by **`+0.262326 ms` mean / `+0.233700 ms` p50** (`25.535488 ms → 25.797814 ms`).
    - At the median (`p50`), **`Δ composite_submit_cpu_ms + Δ tail_wait_cpu_ms = -0.237700 + 0.233700 = -0.004000 ms` (`4.0 microseconds`)**!
  - **Physical Mechanism**:
    - `composite_submit` (`nr_modeled → nr_pre_sr`) executes on the CPU at `t ≈ 0.65..0.94 ms` after `ExecuteCommandLists` entry.
    - At `t ≈ 0.94 ms`, the GPU is still draining the pre-`D0` game queue (`~6.31 ms`) and has ~24.8 ms of GPU work remaining (`D0 → D9 = 20.18 ms`) before `completion` fence is signaled on `game_q`.
