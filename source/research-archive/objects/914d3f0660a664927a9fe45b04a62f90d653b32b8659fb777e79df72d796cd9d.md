## B1. What actually happened (and why P3 missed)

* Both decisive observables landed: **34 → 20 gathers** (7 duplicate
  `skip_gain` + 7 duplicate `proj_gain` removed) and the address arithmetic of
  the blend sites is gone (`add3 12→7`, `shl 118→100`, `mul 253→245`,
  `or 49→39`, `mov −48`). The mechanism is **implemented**, so this is *not* A.
* I over-predicted the instruction delta. I assumed IGC inlines the whole
  158-instruction helper eight times, so the −107 SPIR-V instructions would
  multiply by eight. The measured −108 says IGC had already shared most of that
  work; only the per-site part (the div/mod chain and the duplicated loads)
  was really replicated. **Corrected statement: −2.0 % of the code, not −15 %.**
* Bonus not predicted: the four `load.slm.d16u32` pair-reads of `shK` disappear
  entirely (`4 → 0`) because the collapsed index makes `shK[shk]` provably the
  same address for the `j` and `j+2` call sites (`shk` offset only depends on
  `32*(j&1) + 128*(j>=4)`), so the compiler CSEs them. `send.slm 43 → 39`.
* `load.ugm.d32x32t.a64.ca.cc` became `.flat[A+0x4]` and one `d32x1t` flat
  offset moved `0x80 → 0x68`: the push-constant descriptor reads consolidated.
  Values are unchanged (golds match bit-exactly).

## B2. Triaged side effect (B-class candidate)

`sync.nop 184 → 204 (+10.9 %)` and `sel +8`. `sync.nop` is a scoreboard wait,
so this is the signature of **the hoisted gain loads lengthening a dependency
chain** rather than a growth in real work. By the card's own rule (P6) this
makes the result **B (implemented with a side effect)** until GpuTime says
otherwise.

Recommended follow-up if the time is flat or worse — physically separate the two
sub-changes (they are already separate in the source):

1. **keep** the address collapse (`blend_sa_base` / `blend_shk_base`);
2. **revert** the gain hoist (keep `skip_gain`/`proj_gain` read inside
   `nr_blend_pair`), which removes the extra live range and should take
   `sync.nop` back to ~184 while keeping the −21/−14 gather win from the
   address/serialisation change… note that this variant keeps 34 gathers, so it
   trades the gather win for the scoreboard win. Measure both.
