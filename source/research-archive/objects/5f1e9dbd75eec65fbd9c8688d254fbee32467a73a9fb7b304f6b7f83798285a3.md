# C32 family norm owner results

Outcome: INDISTINCT. Parent minus candidate: +0.053527 ms/frame; 95% CI [-0.053493, +0.160547]. Three alternating process pairs, 30 samples each arm, all 180 samples retained. No identical rerun to seek significance.

Coverage: F01, seven body passes, F2 and F20. Combined fixed and fb2 frames are byte-identical to the expected outputs. Driver scratch remains zero for all five target pipeline identities; no other pipeline scratch allocation increased. F01/body/F2 shared sizes unchanged; F20 25088 -> 24832 bytes.

Body/F01/F2 math.rsqt 16 -> 8 per native, MOV +14, SEL -16, static instructions -10; DPAS/SLM/UGM/barrier counts unchanged. This verifies arithmetic ownership redistribution, not elimination of the entire norm bridge. F20 uses the previously gated compact coefficient-pair implementation, not the failed distributed norm implementation.

The accepted f20-score-owner baseline stays unchanged. This candidate is retained as a numerical asset; no proven net speed gain is claimed.

The combined measurement cannot separate family contributions. Remaining bottleneck attribution is unresolved: this experiment does not establish whether rsqrt was hidden, whether exchange costs offset it, or whether another stage dominates. These require a separately discriminating diagnosis; no speculative new register allocation is added.
